Three years of automated tugs dragged rocks in from the belt and the fringes. Then the catalog ate its own checksums, and now nobody is certain what is already in orbit — including the comets, whose sealed interiors read warm and will not say why.

You sign on as EVA crew. You cut a rock nobody has named and pile the loot at the drop point until the shuttle comes back.
